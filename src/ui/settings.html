Capabilities

Artifacts
Keep replies as artifacts and open them in their own panel next to your chat.
Draw charts in artifacts

Use skills
Add the skills you switched on (Customize) to the model's instructions in every chat.
Use connectors
Offer the tools of your switched-on connectors (Customize) to the model. Off keeps every chat offline from them.
Try another model when yours is busy
When the model you picked hits a rate limit or does not answer, the reply goes to the best other model for the job, and the reply says which answered.

Computer use Local only

Enable computer use
Allow screenshots, clicks and typing on this computer through Bunny AI. Off refuses every such request.
Let the AI control the desktop
The AI may look at the screen, click, type and press keys to do what you ask. Clicks and typing still ask first in Manual and Accept edits. The AI can also ask you to switch this on when it needs it.

Tools the AI builds

Let the AI build its own tools
When nothing Bunny AI has can do a job, the AI may write a small PowerShell script that becomes a tool. You read and approve every one before it is saved, and each run follows your permission mode.
