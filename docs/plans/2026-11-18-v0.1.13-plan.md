## Row

| Row | What | Owner | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| DEVICE-FULL | Each signed-in device has an access level, set only from Bunny's window. **Chats** is today's level and the default for a new device. **Everything** gives every tool (connectors, computer control, the browser panel, installs, schedules, memory, every capability card) and every setting, the same as Bunny's window. The level is chosen when a device signs in for the first time (the window asks) and can be changed later in Settings → Use Bunny in a browser → Signed-in devices. A plain warning comes before Everything is granted. The level is stored per device, so signing out or turning off device access ends it, and it never applies to a device that hasn't signed in. Two things stay with the window even at Everything: turning device access on or off, and changing a device's own level. Otherwise a device could grant itself, or another device, full access, or lock the window out. | Design draws first, then Builder A builds; Security reviews | HIGH |
