## Fixes

- With **Allow other devices** turned on, the **Open to other devices** line at the top no longer pushes the window down. The row under the chat box (folder, branch, mode, effort, job) and the bottom of the sidebar stay on screen.
- The model chip beside **Send** no longer cuts **Auto** down to "A…". Only a long model name is shortened, at its end.
- On a phone, the model chip is wider, so more of a model's name fits.
