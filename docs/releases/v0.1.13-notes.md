## Full access for your own devices

- Each signed-in phone or computer now has an access level, set only from Bunny's window: **Chats** (the default) or **Everything**. Everything lets that device use every tool and every setting, as if it were Bunny's window.
- Bunny asks which level to give when a device signs in for the first time. You can change it later in **Settings → Desktop app → Use Bunny in a browser → Signed-in devices**. A warning comes first before Everything is given.
- Even at Everything, a device can never turn device access on or off, or change any device's level.
- A device at Everything is signed out after 30 minutes idle (Chats: 2 hours).
- **Activity**, next to each device, lists what it did (tool names and setting changes, never the details), for the last 500 entries or 30 days.
- When a device changes a setting, Bunny's window shows a notice with **See Activity**.
- The line at the top names the devices that have Everything.

## Allow other devices stays on

- **Allow other devices to open Bunny** now stays on after Bunny restarts or updates, until you turn it off. Your passcode is kept only in scrambled form. Devices sign in again after a restart, at Chats.
- On a different network (for example a café's Wi-Fi), Bunny does not open itself after a restart. It says why on the setting's row.
- When Bunny starts in the tray, the tray icon says it is open to other devices.

## Settings

- Settings you change are now kept after a restart. **Start when I sign in** and **Keep the same address after restart** were lost every time before; Quick ask could be switched off by a restart.
- **Keep the same address** keeps the address from the moment you turn it on.
- Each Settings tab shows only its own settings. **Use Bunny in a browser** no longer appears under every tab.
- Six settings moved to where they belong: **Try another model when yours is busy** and **Wait for a first reply** to **Models**; **Keep going on long jobs** and **Longest long job** to **Code**; **Default transcript view** and **Label chats by status** to **Appearance**.
- **Verbose** shows longer step results (up to 8,000 characters).
- Status labels update as soon as you close Settings.

## Look and feel

- 13 new fonts for the interface and replies, built into Bunny so they look the same on your phone: Inter, Source Sans 3, IBM Plex Sans, Atkinson Hyperlegible, Lexend, Nunito, Quicksand, Lora, Literata, Source Serif 4, Noto Serif, JetBrains Mono and IBM Plex Mono. Their licences are under **Settings → About**.
- Changing the theme applies at once everywhere, with no flash: the chat, Settings, the title bar and every page, including Providers and Automation.
- Opening another chat, also in another project, no longer reloads the app. Back and Forward still work, and Ctrl-click opens a chat in a new window.

## Quick ask, redesigned

- A lighter floating bar: buttons appear only when you need them, and the answer opens below it.
- The Stop and Copy buttons now show their icons (they were blank).
- The bar's window is exactly as big as the bar, so it no longer catches clicks meant for what is behind it.
- Esc does one thing at a time: closes a menu, then stops an answer, then closes the bar. **Ctrl+Enter** opens the chat in Bunny.
- **Copied** shows when you copy, and code blocks have their own Copy.
- You can read a long answer while it arrives; **Latest** takes you back to the end.
- The bar follows Bunny's theme and interface font.
- With no model connected, the bar offers **Open Bunny settings**.

## Updating

Copies update on their own (**Restart to update**, or check for updates in **Settings → About**).
