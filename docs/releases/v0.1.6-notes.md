## Your models show up after you connect a provider

- On a fresh install, Bunny lists only free and NVIDIA models by default. If you connected a provider such as Anthropic or OpenAI with your own key, the model picker said **No models available**, with nothing to say why.
- Now Bunny tells you how many models are hidden ("3 models are hidden") and offers **Show all models**, both in the picker and on the empty chat screen. One click lists them and picks one for you.
- **Add a provider** shows only when no provider is connected yet.
- Settings → Models → **Show models** explains that the chat's Show all models button sets it to All.
