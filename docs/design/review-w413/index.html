  • The rows outlined in red are two models, listed five times across providers.
  • Raw ids such as mistralai/mistral-large-4-1t-a52b instead of names, and the descriptions are cut off.
  • No filters: one long list grouped by provider.
  • Using the same model from another provider means hunting for its other row.

How it works

  • Same model, one row. Ids that clearly match are merged, ignoring the company prefix, "-free" and capital letters, plus a short list of known aliases. Two ids that only might be the same model stay as separate rows.
  • Provider order per model. Bunny uses the first provider; the others are the backup line, in your order.
  • Automatic switch (your decision: "Yes, with a note"). A limit, an error or no answer within your wait time (W4-9) moves the reply to the same model on the next provider, then to another model, inside the existing "Try another model when yours is busy" setting. One quiet line says who answered and why. A provider that ran out is crossed out until its limit resets.
  • Free means two things (your note). Free costs nothing. Free daily is free up to a limit that resets every day, which most providers offer. Free only shows both. When a daily limit runs out, that provider shows "Back tomorrow" and Bunny moves to the same model on your next provider. Which providers give a daily allowance, and how much, is checked from each provider's own pages before it is built, not guessed.
  • Sections. Auto, Favourites, Recent, then all models. Filters stay as you left them.
  • Keyboard. Ctrl+' opens, typing searches, arrows move, Enter picks, Ctrl+F marks a favourite.
  • Not covered here. Colour contrast is not checked. Names and abilities come from each provider's model list, so a provider that sends poor data can still show a poor row. The models and providers shown are examples.
