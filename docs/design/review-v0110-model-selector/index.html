Model selector in the composer

Design drawing for the owner's approval. Nothing is built from this page. Every model, provider and number in the pictures is an example, drawn only to show the layout.

v0.1.10Risk: MEDIUM (shared component, no data or security change)Light and dark, desktop and phoneDesign session, 2026-11-18
The owner's words
"I am not happy with the model selector in the chat. 1) Bring the model selector in the chat in the same row as the mic, or send button. Also when clicked on it show a floating selector. Show me the artifact for approval first."
What is proposed, in one paragraph
The model moves out of the small grey row under the box and becomes a chip in the box's own button row, between the microphone and Send. Clicking it opens a floating selector right above the chip, with the whole existing picker inside it (search, filters, Best for, Favourites and Recent, one row per model, Details with the Thinking level, the amber switch line). On a phone the chip gets shorter and the selector is a bottom sheet. The row under the box keeps the mode, effort, job and the hint; the model name leaves it.
What you are asked to decide is at the bottom: five small choices, each with my recommendation.

1. Closed: today beside the proposal

Today the model is the fourth item in a row of small grey controls, and it shares that row with the offline chip and the key hint, which get cut off (the live app shows Auto · NVIDIA: Nemotron 3 Ultra (free) · Enter sends…). In the proposal the model is the last thing before Send, so choosing a model and sending are one movement.

2. Open: the floating selector

Opens upward from the chip, about 420 px wide, the width of the Send side of the composer. Esc or a click outside closes it. Up and down arrows move through the rows, Enter picks, and the search box has the focus when it opens. On a phone it is a bottom sheet with a grab handle and a dimmed page behind it.

3. Open with a search typed

Typing hides Best for, Favourites and Recent and shows the matching models only, with the match marked. The count says how many matched. The first match is selected with the keyboard ring so Enter picks it.

4. Inside the selector: Filters and Details

These two parts are the existing W4-13 picker, unchanged in content (drawn in docs/design/review-w413-final/index.html). Filters opens as a strip under the search box. Details replaces the list inside the selector (on a phone, inside the sheet) and Back returns. Thinking is part of Details and is hidden for a provider whose field Bunny does not know.

Behaviour

  • Chip: provider dot, short model name, a Free tag where the first provider is free, a chevron. With the Auto choice it reads "Auto" with the model that was chosen for the job after it, as the row does today. The full name and provider are in the tooltip and read out by a screen reader.
  • Closing: Esc, a click outside, or picking a model closes it and puts the focus back on the chip.
  • Phone: the chip keeps the dot and about ten characters of the name, then an ellipsis; the sheet covers up to 80% of the screen and scrolls inside; the grab handle or a tap on the dimmed page closes it.
  • Code mode: the row under the box also carries the folder and branch (as in the live app); they stay there. Only the model moves.
  • Nothing new is stored and no request changes. The Thinking choice and the provider order are kept per model on this computer, as now.

Risk: MEDIUM

  • Shared component: the picker is used from the composer in Chat and Code, so both need the walk (open, search, filter, Details, Thinking, pick, Esc, phone). The existing end-to-end tests for the picker (e2e/w4-13-picker.spec.js) will need the new trigger and the sheet.
  • No security control, schema or permission changes. No change to what is sent to a provider.
  • Focus handling is the likeliest source of bugs: a picker that opens above the box and takes the focus must give it back, and must not close when the on-screen keyboard appears on a phone.

Decisions for the owner (my recommendation first)

  1. Chip text on desktop. Show the model's short name (recommended), or only the dot and an icon to save width.
  2. Free tag in the chip. Show it (recommended, it is what people check), or leave it to the selector.
  3. Opening direction. Always upward (recommended, because the composer is at the bottom of the window); the selector never needs to open downward.
  4. Phone shape. Bottom sheet (recommended), or a full-screen page.
  5. Under-row. Keep mode, effort and job there (recommended), or move effort next to the chip as well.

Not drawn: the picker's empty state when no provider is connected, and the "Show all models" button; both stay as they are today inside the selector.
