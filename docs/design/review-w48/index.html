W4-8: the quick-ask bar, today beside proposed

Design session, 2026-11-16. A global shortcut (default Ctrl+Alt+Space) opens a small floating bar over any app: the bunny mark, a prompt box, Enter to send, Esc to close. Sending opens Bunny's window on a new chat. Drawn: closed hint, empty, typing, the + menu, a screenshot chip, sending, and the Settings row (on, changing, shortcut taken, off). Light and dark over one busy invented desktop.

Not covered, stated plainly: no contrast ratios measured (UNKNOWN); the desktop, windows and files in the pictures are invented scenery; the only "today" picture is the Desktop app section of Settings (shot at 800 px wide on an invented profile with the section unhidden, since it is shown only inside the desktop app); the real Windows tray and the real shortcut registration are not drawn or tested; the look is Bunny's own tokens and does not copy any other product's bar.
