Settings, made clearer

v0.1.15 · SETTINGS-OVERHAUL · from QA's audit of every control (docs/qa/settings-audit-e2ec6ec6.md, 1bd3bfe7) · invented profile only

For approval

In short

QA found that nothing is dead and everything sticks. The problems are where things sit and how clearly they are said. Proposed: 14 sections become 12, each with one job; Quick ask gets one home; every setting has a help line; everything saves as you change it with a small "Saved" mark; and wrong input gets a clear message instead of being dropped.

Two decisions for the Chief of Staff and the owner
1 · Language. Only about a third of the main window's words change, and Settings and replies stay English. Choose: (a) keep the picker with an honest line ("Changes about a third of the main window so far; Settings and replies stay English"), or (b) hide it until the translation is finished. Drawn as (a).
2 · Google sign-in. Today the button is greyed because a person must register their own Google app. Choose: (a) an honest note and a short "How to connect" steps panel (drawn), or (b) hide Google until Bunny can offer a one-click sign-in.

3 · One way of saving, and clear errors

Every setting saves as you change it, switches and menus at once, text boxes when you leave them or press Enter. Next to the control a small Saved appears. There is no Save button anywhere (the Models section's "Save models" goes). When the input is wrong, nothing is saved and the line says why, in red, right under it.
