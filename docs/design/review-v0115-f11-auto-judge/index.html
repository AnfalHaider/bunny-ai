In short

In Auto, a second, cheaper model checks each risky action before it runs. Safe actions go through with no card. Risky ones (sending secrets out, deleting many files, and the like) are blocked even when no rule asks. If it blocks too often, or can't be reached, Auto goes back to asking with cards, and says so in the chat.

  • Skips the check: reads, and edits inside your project folder. Goes to the check: commands, connector write tools, computer and browser actions, downloads, installs.
  • Still always asks: sending email or messages, building a tool, installing from GitHub, anything your own Deny or Ask rule covers. Deny always wins.
  • The check sees your own messages, your trusted project instructions and the action itself. It never sees web pages or file text I read, so hostile content cannot steer it.
  • No answer within 20 seconds counts as blocked. Three blocks in a row, or 20 in a reply, switch to cards for the rest of that reply.
  • Nothing is a banner: every sign is a small mark on a step or one line in the thread.
