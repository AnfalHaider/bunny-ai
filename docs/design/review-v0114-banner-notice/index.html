In short

Superseded 2026-11-19. The owner decided: no banner on top, anywhere. Only the chip stays (windows, phones and browser pages). The notification drawn below is no longer wanted; the chip is. See the OPEN-SIGNIN and PHONE-LAYOUT pages.

Security's principle: the notice may go away, but the state never does. The banner and the device's line can be dismissed. A small status chip stays in the window's top bar for as long as access is open, and a device page at Everything keeps a small "Everything" chip. A click on the window's chip brings the banner back. Nothing about a dismissal is saved anywhere: a reload or restart shows the banner again.
