The "open to other devices" banner becomes a notification

v0.1.14 · BANNER-NOTICE · drawn for the owner's request of 2026-11-19 · invented data only

For approval

What I drew, in short

Today the amber banner takes a full row across the top of Bunny's window for as long as device access is on, and a coloured line does the same on the device's own page. Proposed: the loud part, the banner, becomes a notification you can dismiss. What stays is one small chip that cannot be dismissed, so the open state is always findable (this keeps RS-5 and R-DF1 in spirit: open and Everything are never hidden, only the big bar goes). The chip turns red when a device has Everything. A notification comes back by itself when something new happens.

Security decides the rule. Everything below is a proposal for them to accept or change. The table marks what may be dismissed and what must stay.

Questions for Security

  2. Should the Everything notification come back by itself (for example after 10 minutes), or only on a new event?
  3. Should "dismissed" last until the window is restarted (proposed), or forever for one device?
