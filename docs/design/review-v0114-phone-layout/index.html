In short

Two things run off the screen on a phone, and both get a familiar fix. The top bar shows eight controls (Export as a word, seven icons) plus the new devices chip, so the right half is cut off and the chat title is pushed out entirely. Settings shows 16 sections as one sideways strip, with only three or four visible and a name cut in half.

What the ⋯ holds

Phone: a bottom sheet, rows of 44 px, in the order the bar has today. "Open in its own window" is not offered on a phone, since it opens a second window the phone cannot show.

720 px: a small menu under the button, with what the bar did not have room for.

The chip tapped

The chip opens a panel under the bar, 12 px in from each side: who is signed in at which level, Turn off, and the way to Settings. (Proposed.)

The title is the one thing that always keeps room: it is the middle, takes what is left, and shortens with "…". The chip shortens its words first ("Open · 1", "Everything"), never leaves. There is no banner or notice anywhere (the owner's decision of 2026-11-19, Security's BN-6 to BN-9): only the chip. A new sign-in, an Everything grant, a reopened route or a setting changed from a device switches the chip to the event's words ("New sign-in", "Setting changed" with a count) until it is clicked; on a narrow phone the short words are used, at 720 px the full ones. Pick them under "Chip" above. A click opens the panel below, with the devices list or the device's Activity.

On a device's own page there is no notice at all: at Everything a red "Everything" chip in its header, at Chats nothing.
