In short

The owner: "improve the layout of plugins and connections, skill etc, tighten the rows. The switch should be in front of each, not under it." Today a connector is a 95 px row with a column of words (Type, Status, Test, Trust read-only labels, Remove) and, on a phone, a 160 px card with the switch on a line of its own under the name. Proposed: one row of about 52 px per item, the same density as the Engines page: logo, name with a one-line dim description, the switch on the same line, and everything else folded into a ⋯ menu. Words that only say "Not checked" go away; a status shows only when something needs you.
